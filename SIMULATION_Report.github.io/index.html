Methodology

The project began with PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization) but migrated to SAC (Soft Actor-Critic) for the final pipeline. The transition was driven by five key factors:

Sample Efficiency

SAC is off-policy. It stores every experience in a 1M-capacity replay buffer and reuses them for multiple gradient updates. PPO discards data after one update pass. SAC learns to walk in far fewer environment steps.

GPU Utilization

SAC repeatedly samples training batches from the replay buffer and performs dense tensor ops. This keeps an NVIDIA GPU saturated. PPO's collect-once, update-once design is more CPU-bound and underutilizes GPU throughput.

Exploration

SAC incorporates entropy maximization directly into the reward function. The agent is rewarded for maintaining uncertainty, forcing wide exploration. PPO relies on fixed noise schedules and converges to premature local optima.

Continuous Control

SAC's twin-Q architecture mitigates value overestimation bias in continuous action spaces. This is critical for Humanoid's 17-DoF action space. PPO's clipped surrogate is simpler but less effective for high-DoF locomotion.

Empirical Results

PPO Humanoid-v5 plateaued at 250K timesteps with marginal walking quality. SAC Humanoid-v4 scaled cleanly to 800K timesteps with visibly more fluid gait at every checkpoint — from stumbling (200K) to stable walking (800K).

SAC is an off-policy actor-critic algorithm with three key innovations:

Actor (Policy Network)

Maps observations to action distributions. Updated to maximize both expected return and entropy, balancing exploitation with exploration.

Twin Critics (Q₁, Q₂)

Two Q-networks reduce overestimation bias by taking the minimum of their outputs. Target networks use soft Polyak averaging (τ = 0.005) for stable learning.

Entropy Regularization

Temperature α is learned automatically. High α drives exploration early in training; lower α narrows the policy as skill improves.

Replay Buffer

FIFO buffer of 1,000,000 transitions (s, a, r, s′). Batches of 256 are sampled uniformly for each gradient step.

Key files in the project:

train_sac.py

Main SAC training loop. Creates environment, initialises model, trains in 25K-step chunks with periodic saves.

render_checkpoints.py

Loads a trained SAC checkpoint, runs 1000 environment steps, outputs 1080p MP4.

train_interactive.py

Interactive training with pause/resume/restart controls via keyboard input, plus periodic evaluation and preview rendering.

aesthetic_creatures/

Modular v3.0 pipeline: envs (50+ specs), models (PPO builder), recording (trajectory capture), rendering (art generation + video export), scripts (train + replay CLI).

Training Progression

Four checkpoints from the SAC training run. Each video is 1000 frames (≈33 s) at 1920×1080. Watch the gait stabilise from early stumbling to fluid locomotion.

200K

Early gait — rough, unstable, limited coordination. The agent is still learning basic joint synchronisation.

400K

Mid-training — improved balance, more fluid motion, fewer falls. The policy is converging on a stable gait pattern.

600K

Late training — stable walking emerges. Smoother transitions between stride phases and better energy efficiency.

800K

Final checkpoint — the most refined locomotion. Consistent, balanced, and efficient walking gait.
